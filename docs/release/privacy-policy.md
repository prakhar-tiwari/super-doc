# Super Doc privacy policy

*Last updated: 22 October 2026*

Super Doc is made for writing notes and keeping documents on your own phone.

**What stays on your phone.** Everything you write, photograph, scan, record by voice, or change
with the AI helper is stored only inside the app on your phone. We do not have servers, accounts,
analytics, or advertising, and the app does not send your documents or notes to us or anyone else.

**Voice typing and reading aloud** use your phone's own speech services. By default the app asks
them to work offline. The microphone is used only after you tap a "Speak" button, while it is listening.

**The optional AI helper** runs entirely on the phone. If you choose to download it, the app
fetches a model file over a secure (HTTPS) connection from Hugging Face (huggingface.co and its
download servers), checks it with a SHA-256 checksum, and keeps it on the phone. Only the download request is sent;
none of your text is.

**Scanning and reading text in photos** use Google's ML Kit through Google Play services on your
phone. Google may collect anonymous diagnostic information about how these features perform;
see Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

**Sending and backups happen only when you choose.** When you tap Send, the file goes to the app
you pick (for example WhatsApp). When you save a backup, it is one file that you place where you
choose (for example Google Drive). Anyone who has that file can read your documents, so keep it safe.

**Deleting.** Deleted items go to the Recycle Bin for 30 days and are then removed. Uninstalling the
app removes everything it stored.

**Children.** The app is meant for adults.
